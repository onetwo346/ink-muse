about the artist

Ink is patience.
Cover-ups are craft.

Every piece that comes through the door has a story — and sometimes that story needs a new chapter. Whether you're freshening faded lines, reclaiming skin from a tattoo that no longer fits, or starting completely fresh, the work here is intentional, unhurried, and built to last.

Specialising in touch-ups and cover-up transformations, with a keen eye for matching existing styles or introducing something entirely new.

what we do

Every kind of
transformation

From subtle refreshes to complete reimaginings — the studio specialises in work that demands a skilled eye and a steady hand.

Touch-Ups

Faded colour, softened lines, and old ink that's lost its punch. We restore existing tattoos to their original intention — or better.

Cover-Ups

Turning what was into something new. Whether it's a name, an old design, or a regret — we build a piece that hides the past while standing beautifully on its own.

Custom Design

Collaborative from the first sketch. We work through your reference, your skin tone, and your placement to design something made only for you.
